About

I'm a Senior Data Analyst with more than 18 years of experience spanning NGOs, higher education, financial technology, and e-commerce. My focus is the full path from raw source to clear answer: extracting, cleaning, transforming, and visualizing data so teams can confidently answer the questions that matter.

I work hands-on across databases like PostgreSQL, Vertica, BigQuery, and Snowflake, and with business intelligence tools including Power BI, Tableau, and Domo. I have 8 years of experience with SQL and 4 with Python for aggregating, manipulating, and visualizing data, and I am increasingly applying modern AI tooling (Snowflake Cortex, fine-tuned LLMs) to text and prediction problems.
